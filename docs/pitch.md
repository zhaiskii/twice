# Pitch notes

## Founder's framing (keep)

> "Guys, come on — in 24 hours you cannot build something that scales to $100M.
> So let's scale your working product."

i.e. Twice is a working wedge that plugs into a product that already has
distribution (Zimran's Subpilot / Finelo funnels): Subpilot cancels and
negotiates; Twice adds *replace and dedupe* + affiliate revenue per switch.

DECISION(standalone-b2c): the product itself stays a standalone B2C web app
(B2B is an automatic fail). The "scale your product" line is the closing
argument and Q&A answer, not the product definition.
